\paragraph{Shared versus separate models.}\label{app:shared-vs-separate}
To isolate the effect of joint training, we train one DiTXA-L/2 on both directions with
the controlled recipe of Table~1 (App.~G): the same data, periodic SDE, batch size 512,
learning-rate schedule, EMA, image REPA, and conditioning dropout. The only change is
that both directional losses are enabled. Each optimizer step sums the T2I and I2T losses
on the same batch. We evaluate this shared model with the Table~1 protocol at two
budgets. At \emph{200K} steps it matches the specialists' combined forward/backward
compute and the number of training examples each direction sees. At \emph{400K} steps it
matches the specialists' combined number of optimizer steps and sampled image--caption
pairs, at about twice their combined compute. The shared model has two direction-specific
conditioning embedders and REPA heads, a difference of under $0.5\%$ of the
${\sim}700$M trunk parameters. It saves about half the storage of deploying two
specialists.
